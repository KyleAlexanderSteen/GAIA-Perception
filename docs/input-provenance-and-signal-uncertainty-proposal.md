# Input Provenance and Signal Uncertainty — Perception Proposal

**Status:** Proposed; not approved or implemented.
**Canonical proposal:** https://github.com/KyleAlexanderSteen/GAIA-Documentation/blob/docs/alignment-attunement-self-improvement/GAIA-Documentation/GAIA-Universal-Principles-Integrity-Knowledge-and-Flourishing.md

## Candidate requirements

- Preserve input modality, source/origin, capture or receipt time, transformation history, and applicable consent/rights restrictions.
- Distinguish raw inputs from extracted text, transcriptions, classifications, summaries, and inferred intent.
- Represent ambiguity, low signal quality, missing context, and competing interpretations rather than silently converting them into certainty.
- Preserve links from derived outputs back to the input segments or evidence that support them, subject to privacy policy.
- Avoid treating model-generated captions, OCR, transcription, or classifications as ground truth without validation appropriate to risk.
- Apply privacy minimization, access controls, retention/deletion, and sensitive-content handling throughout the pipeline.
- Support corrections and invalidation when the source input or extraction result is corrected.

## Verification

Evaluate modality-specific error rates, uncertainty behavior, source linkage, corrupted/ambiguous inputs, conflicting interpretations, privacy redaction, and correction propagation. No perception capability is asserted by this proposal.
