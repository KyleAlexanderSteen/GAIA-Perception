# Ecological Observation Quality and Uncertainty — Proposal

**Status:** Proposed; not approved or implemented.

## Candidate requirements

For satellite, aerial, sensor, and field inputs used in ecosystem digital twins, preserve source and acquisition time, spatial/temporal resolution, coordinate reference system, preprocessing, model/data versions, access and licensing restrictions, and validation provenance.

Separate direct observations, sensor-derived features, model classifications, predictions, scenario assumptions, and human recommendations. A high-resolution detection is not automatically ground truth, and an output can be precise in geometry while uncertain in ecological meaning.

Record uncertainty and known blind spots by geography, season, sensor conditions, land cover, and training-data coverage. Validate against appropriate expert annotations and field observations; measure false positives/negatives and missing-data effects. Support corrections and invalidation when source data or derived classifications change.

Protect sensitive species locations and community/landholder data where disclosure creates harm. Keep ecological interventions advisory until domain experts and authorized stakeholders review the evidence and likely impacts.

This proposal adds no perception implementation and does not authorize ecological interventions.
